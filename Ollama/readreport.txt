Summarise the report 
 
Here's a summary of the report based solely on the provided text:

The report, titled “Growing back better: putting nature and net zero at the heart of the economic recovery,” is the Third Report of the House of Commons Environmental Audit Committee. It examines how UK government policies and programs contribute to environmental protection and sustainable development following the COVID-19 pandemic and resulting economic impacts.

The Committee received over 100 submissions of written evidence and held hearings with economists, business groups, the Bank of England, and conservation groups. They also questioned businesses receiving public financial support. 25 witnesses (11 women, 14 men) gave evidence. 

The report considered evidence gathered before the publication of the Ten Point Plan and National Infrastructure Strategy (November 2020) and the Energy White Paper (January 2021), but held a final hearing after the Autumn Spending Review. It focuses on ecological crisis, economic recovery, net zero emissions, and zoonotic diseases. Correspondence with the Governor of the Bank of England will be appended to the report, and embargoed copies will be made available.

What are the main goals of the report? 
 
Based on the provided documents, the main goals of the report are:

*   To examine how to “green” the economic recovery following the COVID-19 pandemic.
*   To assess whether government policies and programs contribute to environmental protection and sustainable development.
*   To ensure economic recovery measures are consistent with commitments under the Paris Agreement and Convention on Biological Diversity, and the net zero target by 2050.
*   To examine the connections between human health and natural systems.
*   To discuss the implications of the pandemic for carbon emissions, clean technology investment and international climate negotiations.
